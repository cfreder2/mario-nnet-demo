Mario Death Map

Two screens for an event table. One lets people play World 1-1 and maps every death. The other shows an AI teaching itself the same level.

Screen 1: Where does Mario die?Play, die, and watch your death join everyone else's on the heatmap. Screen 2: An AI learns 1-1Five trials at once and the neural network growing as it learns.

Hidden keys

Ctrl+Alt+MScreen 1: sound on/off
Ctrl+Alt+EScreen 1: download every event as CSV
Ctrl+Alt+R twiceScreen 1: archive player deaths and start a fresh map (nothing is deleted; it moves to data/archive/)
Ctrl+Alt+TScreen 1: back to the title screen
Ctrl+Alt+1–4Screen 2: on-screen speed 1×, 2×, 4×, 8×
Ctrl+Alt+HScreen 2: background helpers on/off (off = every attempt plays on screen, slower learning)
Ctrl+Alt+N twiceScreen 2: throw the brain away and learn from nothing
Ctrl+Alt+FFull screen
